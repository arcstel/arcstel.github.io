// about

Security that holds up to an audit

I'm an Identity & Access Management analyst focused on the full identity lifecycle — from the moment a joiner is onboarded to the day a leaver's access goes dark. My work sits at the intersection of security, compliance, and clinical operations: enforcing least privilege without breaking the workflows people depend on.

I've administered SailPoint IGA and One Identity Manager, run access audits across enterprise systems, supported privileged access management initiatives, and delivered audit-ready evidence for SOX, HIPAA, and NIST-aligned controls. Before security, I was a Navy corpsman and an LPN — which is where I learned how much the human side of access actually matters.

Audit-ready by default

Access reviews, recertification, evidence, and SOPs that stand up to SOX/HIPAA scrutiny.

Least privilege, enforced

RBAC/ABAC, role governance, and SoD controls that reduce standing access.

Healthcare fluency

Epic, Imprivata, Voalte, Citrix, and clinical workflow-aware provisioning.

Automation-minded

PowerShell, SQL, and reporting to replace manual access churn.

Identity Attack Path Analyzer

An interactive identity-governance app that models identities, groups, roles, entitlements, and systems as a graph — then traces how any identity reaches a crown-jewel system. Surfaces dormant and orphaned accounts, privilege creep, MFA gaps, and segregation-of-duties conflicts, and exports audit evidence.
