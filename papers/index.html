Published draft

Topological Field Arithmetic: Foundations, Obstructions, and a Harmonic-Analysis Reframing

William Paul Sindle · September 2026 · Concept paper with a rigorous negative core
topology number theory harmonic analysis adelic philosophy of math

Reinterprets arithmetic as the dynamics of excitations in a topological medium. Proves two obstructions — a representation obstruction (any faithful model is isomorphic to arithmetic) and a no-go for continuously-emergent constants — and identifies the rigorous content of the idea in Fourier/Pontryagin duality, the Hardy–Littlewood circle method, and Tate's adelic analysis.
